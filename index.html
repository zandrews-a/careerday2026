Dispatch

Two lines merge into one junction. Get every train to the right yard without putting two of them in the junction at the same time.

  • HoldTap a signal — on the track or in the bar below. Red holds, green releases.
  • Line the switchTap the yard the next train is headed to — on the track or in the bar.
  • Don't collideTwo trains in the junction ends your shift.
  • Warm-upYour first 20 seconds don't count against you.
